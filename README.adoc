= DiabolicUI for Wotlk 3.3.5

Forked from https://github.com/Hrishi29x/DiabolicUI

Well met, traveler. I am but a humble tinkerer who took DiabolicUI, forked it for 3.3.5, and mended what small things I could along my travels through Northrend. Nothing grand, just a few fixes and features gathered on the road and shared freely, should they serve you as well as they have served me. The full list lies below.

== Recommended Addons

* https://github.com/bkader/Masque-WoTLK[Masque] - to skin the buttons of addons

== Features

=== Unit Frames

* Reputation bar takes the place of the experience bar when a reputation is tracked.
* Role icons (tank / healer / damage) next to party and raid frames.
* Optional animated 3D model portrait on party and focus frames, sitting above the health bar with matching border/backdrop/hover-glow chrome (`Show Portrait`).
* Class-color option for player, target, party, raid and tab-target-of-target health bars (`Show class colors`).
* Weapon enchants (temporary buffs from poisons, oils, stones, etc.) shown as auras on the player frame.
* Mind-controlled enemies and vehicles correctly display their orb/frame.
* `Toggle Fake Party` / `Toggle Fake Raid` buttons preview the party/raid frame styling without needing a real group.

=== Action Bars & Micro Menu

* Number of action bars and side bars configurable with `/diabolic num_bars [1,2,3]` and `/diabolic num_side_bars [0,1,2]`.
* Gold, FPS and latency shown next to the micro-menu button, each independently toggleable (`Show Gold`, `Show FPS & Latency`); gold uses proper coin icons instead of colored letters.
* Help Request menu reintroduced.

=== Chat

* Updated layout, with options grouped into "Appearance" (background opacity, fade chat, time fading, time visible) and "Miscellaneous".
* `Copy Web Links`: left-click a web link (http:// or https://) in chat to open a popup with it selected and ready to copy.
* Chat bubble and damage fonts set to Exocet - best results paired with https://github.com/Stormhand-dev/Lexara---HD-Font-Renderer-for-WoW-3.3.5.

=== Tooltips

* Tooltip follows the cursor.
* Configurable anchor point, horizontal and vertical offset.

=== Minimap

* Built in as a module - no separate `DiabolicUI-Minimap` download needed.
* Reskinned border, clock, zone text and mail icon, edit mode, and draggable buttons.
* Zone text hugs whichever screen edge the minimap is currently on, and cross-fades on zone change (`Animate Zone Text`, with configurable fade-out/fade-in durations).

=== Objectives

* `Fade Quest Tracker`: fades Questie's tracker out after it hasn't been moused over for a while, with configurable delay (`Time Fading`) and faded `Opacity`.

NOTE: DiabolicUI does not disable Blizzard's own objective tracker - that's `Questie`'s job, and it already takes care of it when installed.

=== Options

Everything above lives under a single "DiabolicUI" category in Interface Options, with dedicated *Units*, *Tooltips*, *Chat* and *Minimap* submenus for the settings that belong to each.

=== Misc

* WorldMap disabled in favor of the bundled minimap module.
* Dropped compatibility code for other WoW versions - this fork targets WotLK 3.3.5 only, keeping the codebase smaller and simpler.
