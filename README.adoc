= DiabolicUI for Wotlk 3.3.5

Forked from https://github.com/Hrishi29x/DiabolicUI

Well met, traveler. I am but a humble tinkerer who took DiabolicUI, forked it for 3.3.5, and mended what small things I could along my travels through Northrend. Nothing grand, just a few fixes and features gathered on the road and shared freely, should they serve you as well as they have served me. The full list lies below.

== Getting Started

. Put the `DiabolicUI` folder in `Interface/AddOns/` and log in.
. Open the options with `/dui config` (or Interface Options -> AddOns -> DiabolicUI). Every setting is there, on these pages: *DiabolicUI* (menu, objectives, loot), *Tooltips*, *Units*, *Chat*, *Messages*, *Specializations* and *AddOns*.

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Command |Does
|`/dui config` |opens the options
|`/dui num_bars 1-3`, `/dui num_side_bars 0-2` |number of bottom bars and side bars
|`/dui tracker`, `/dui tracker reset` |unlocks/locks Blizzard's objectives tracker to move it, or puts it back
|`/dui autoposition` |turns the automatic chat window position on or off
|`/dui setscale <number>` |UI scale
|`/dui resetsetup` |puts the chat window back in its default place
|===

`/diabolic` and `/diabolicui` work too.

== Features

=== Action Bars

* One to three bottom bars and up to two side bars, between the Diablo III health and mana orbs.
* *Talent specialization button* on the skull, once you've learned Dual Talent Specialization: one click switches spec (`Show Specialization Switch`).
* *Specializations* page: for each spec, what happens when you switch to it - an equipment set, an Action Bar Saver profile, the number of bars and side bars, and two of your macros: one run by the click that switches ("before"), one run once the switch is done (the skull glows, click it). Macros can do anything, `/use` and `/cast` included.
* *Stance button* on the angel: shows your active form, presence, aura or stance, and opens the stance bar. With a single form (Shadowform, Stealth) it is that form's button.
* Micro menu with the Help Request menu, and gold, FPS and latency next to it (`Show Gold`, `Show FPS & Latency`, coin icons for gold). The Social button's tooltip lists your online guild members and friends.
* Experience bar, replaced by the reputation bar while you watch a faction.

=== Unit Frames

* Player, target, focus, pet, target of target, party and raid frames. Raid frames are grouped by party, with a "Group N" title over each group.
* Role icons (tank, healer, damage) on party and raid frames.
* Class colors on health bars (`Show class colors`) and animated 3D portraits on party and focus frames (`Show Portrait`).
* Weapon enchants (poisons, oils, stones) shown as auras on the player frame.
* Death knight runes as glyphs above the action bars, colored by type and filling up while they recharge.
* `Toggle Fake Party` / `Toggle Fake Raid` preview the party and raid frames without a group.

=== Chat

* Styled chat windows that fade out after a while (`Fade Chat`, `Time Fading`, `Time Visible`), with a background while typing (`Background Opacity`).
* Web links (http, www, e-mail) become clickable: a click opens them selected in a box, ready to copy (`Copy Web Links`).
* *Messages* page: shorter system lines - loot, money, experience, reputation, honor, quests, away/busy, friends online, auctions, achievements - and hidden channel join/leave notices, each on its own switch.
* New lines can slide in instead of jumping (`Slide New Messages`, off by default).
* Chat bubbles in a bigger font on a dark backdrop. Chat bubble and damage fonts in Exocet - best paired with https://github.com/Stormhand-dev/Lexara---HD-Font-Renderer-for-WoW-3.3.5[Lexara].

=== Tooltips

* Tooltips follow the cursor, with a configurable anchor point and offsets.
* Diablo III style item tooltips (`Reskin Tooltip`): the name on a banner in its quality color, the icon beside the text, damage per second or armor as a big number, sell price and durability at the bottom. Room is made for the transmog lines of the ChromieCraft client patch.

=== Minimap

* Built in: no separate `DiabolicUI-Minimap` download needed.
* Styled border, clock, zone text, mail and Dungeon Finder buttons, movable with draggable buttons.
* The zone text follows the side of the screen the minimap is on, and cross-fades on zone change (`Animate Zone Text`).

=== Quests & Objectives

* `Fade Quest Tracker`: fades the tracker (Questie's, or Blizzard's) after you stop hovering it (`Time Fading`, `Opacity`).
* Blizzard's objectives tracker in Exocet and DiabolicUI's colors (`Style Objectives Tracker`), and movable (`Unlock Tracker` on the main page).
* Error and quest progress messages on DiabolicUI's own display, with repeated ones dropped.
* Battleground and world PvP scores and capture bars in DiabolicUI's style.

NOTE: DiabolicUI doesn't hide Blizzard's tracker: Questie does that when it's installed, and Questie's tracker has its own font settings (DiabolicUI's fonts are available there).

=== Blizzard Frames

* Popups and the Dungeon Finder (queue tooltip, role check, ready check) in DiabolicUI's frames; the Escape menu with big Diablo buttons.
* Bag and bank slots with quality-colored borders, item levels on gear and greyed out junk.
* Character, spellbook, merchant, world map and Dungeon Finder windows tinted in DiabolicUI's colors.
* Optional custom loot window (`Reskin Loot Window`).
* World map zone names in Exocet (`Style World Map`), working alongside Mapster.
* Nameplates in DiabolicUI's style, unless a nameplate addon such as Kui Nameplates is installed.

=== Other Addons (*AddOns* page)

* `Style TrinketMenu`: TrinketMenu's buttons look like the action bars' buttons, without backgrounds.

== Recommended Addons

* https://github.com/bkader/Masque-WoTLK[Masque] and https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/masque-diabolic[Masque_Diabolic] - Diablo-style buttons for other addons, such as Bagnon
* https://github.com/bkader/SharedMedia[SharedMedia] and https://github.com/GoldpawsStuff/SharedMedia_ExocetBlizzard[SharedMedia_ExocetBlizzard] - the Diablo font in other addons
* https://github.com/bkader/KuiNameplates-WoTLK[Kui_Nameplates] - nameplates
* https://github.com/paokkerkir/Mapster[Mapster] - world map
* https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/mbb/files/all?page=1&pageSize=20&version=3.3.5&showAlphaFiles=hide[MBB] - minimap button bag collector
* https://github.com/sloan-008/Questie-335[Questie] - quests and quest log
* Action Bar Saver - action bar profiles for the Specializations page
* TrinketMenu - trinket switching, styled by DiabolicUI

This fork targets WotLK 3.3.5 only: code for other WoW versions was dropped to keep it small and simple.

== Development

The Lua code is covered by an offline test suite (LuaUnit, plain Lua 5.1 with fake WoW APIs, no game client needed) - see `tests/README.md` for how it works and the https://github.com/apt-get-update/DiabolicUI/tree/master/docs[developer documentation] for the architecture.

[source,bash]
----
tests/run_all.sh                 # run every test
tests/coverage/run.sh            # run them with line coverage
tests/coverage/run.sh --readme   # ...and refresh the table below
----
