== ⚔️ Action bars

One module, `ActionBars`, with one widget per file. Buttons come from the
`ActionButton` handler.

|===
| File | Widget

| `elements/bar1.lua` … `bar5.lua` | The main bar, two more bottom bars, two side bars
| `elements/pet.lua`, `stance.lua`, `vehicle.lua` | Pet, stance and vehicle bars
| `elements/floaters.lua` | Stance toggle, vehicle and taxi exit buttons
| `elements/specswitch.lua` | The spec switch on the skull, and each spec's commands
| `elements/menu.lua` | The micro menu, friends and guild on the social button
| `elements/xp.lua`, `reputation.lua` | Experience and reputation bars
| `elements/artwork.lua` | The bars' art
| `elements/keybinds.lua` | Key bindings copied from Blizzard's bars
| `controllers/`, `templates/` | Secure anchor frames, shared button and flyout templates
|===

A button's icon color follows its state, checked in this order:

|===
| State | When | Icon

| `taxi` | Dead or a ghost | Grey
| `mana` | Not enough mana | Blue
| `unusable` | Can't be used | Dark
| `range` | Target out of range | Red
| `usable` | Anything else | Normal
|===

== ❤️ Unit frames

The `UnitFrame` handler builds them,
link:{{< relref "unitframe-elements" >}}[elements] fill them.

|===
| File (`units/`) | Frame

| `player.lua` | The health and power orbs
| `target.lua`, `tot.lua`, `focus.lua`, `pet.lua` | Target, its target, focus, pet
| `party.lua`, `raid.lua` | Group frames
| `enemies.lua` | Boss and arena frames
| `shared.lua` | What the small frames share
| `testmode.lua` | A fake roster to preview party and raid layouts
|===

== 🏷️ Nameplates

`nameplates.lua` rebuilds 3.3.5's plates (health, level, name, icons, casts);
`auras.lua` adds your auras over them. Each plate is a frame with a shared
metatable (`+NamePlate_WotLK_MT+`). 3.3.5 plates have no unit: a plate learns
its unit's GUID while it's the target or mouseover.

== 🪟 Blizzard

Small independent modules, each restyling or replacing one Blizzard piece.
Most windows are built from the parts in `windows.lua`; their sizes, art and
places live in `settings/blizzard.lua`.

++++
{{< mermaid >}}
flowchart LR
    st["⚙️ settings/blizzard.lua"] --> w["🪟 windows.lua (stone, iron, red band, plate, tabs, buttons)"]
    w --> own["📄 one file per window (character, spellbook, mail...)"]
    w --> pn["📋 panels.lua (every other window, from a table)"]
    own --> la["🔍 layout-scan.lua (/dui layout)"]
    pn --> la
{{< /mermaid >}}
++++

|===
| File | Does

| `windows.lua` | The window kit: stone, iron, red band, title plate, tabs, buttons, panels (below)
| `panels.lua` | Every window listed in `panels` in `settings/blizzard.lua`
| `character.lua`, `spellbook.lua`, `mail.lua`, `auction.lua`... | One file per window that needs more than that table
| `layout-scan.lua` | `/dui layout`: saves the open windows' parts and art for the link:{{< relref "windows-editor" >}}[editor]
| `tooltips.lua`, `itemtooltips.lua`, `spelltooltips.lua` | Tooltips and menus
| `lootframe.lua`, `rollwindow.lua`, `lootpreview.lua` | Loot (link:{{< relref "case-study-loot-window" >}}[case study], link:{{< relref "raid-rolls" >}}[Raid Rolls])
| `adibags.lua`, `immersion.lua`, `trinketmenu.lua`... | Other addons in our look
|===

The rest are small, one Blizzard piece each, named after it (`gamemenu.lua`,
`popups.lua`, `worldmap.lua`...). To restyle a window, see
link:{{< relref "common-tasks#restyle-a-blizzard-window" >}}[Common Tasks].

=== The window kit

`modules/blizzard/windows.lua` (the module `Blizzard: Windows`, called `kit` in
the window files). The calls you'll use most:

|===
| Call | Does

| `CreateWindow(frame, insets)` | The stone window in its iron frame, around a Blizzard frame
| `SetTitle(header, text)` | The title on its plate
| `StyleTab`, `HangTabs` | Tabs as hanging banners
| `StyleButtons(frame)` | Every button in it red, with one font that fits them all
| `StyleDropDown`, `StyleEditBox`, `StyleCheckBox`, `StyleScrollBars` | Each control in our look
| `AddPanel`, `AddZone`, `AddParchment` | Slate, leather or parchment behind a part
| `LineBand(header, parts)` | Parts in the red band
| `StyleExpanders`, `LightRow` | A list's +/− buttons and its chosen row
|===

Moving Blizzard's secure frames in combat causes taint: wrap that code with
`Module:Wrap` (link:{{< relref "engine-architecture#events" >}}[Engine]).

== 💬 Chat

|===
| File | Module | Does

| `windows.lua` | `ChatWindows` | Window style, tab fading
| `bubbles.lua` | `ChatBubbles` | Speech bubbles over heads
| `filters.lua` | `ChatFilters` | Name and channel links, clickable web links
| `cleaner.lua` | `ChatCleaner` | Shorter loot, money, experience... lines
| `slide.lua` | `ChatSlide` | New lines slide in
|===

Web links use our own `url` link type, so `filters.lua` overrides
`SetItemRef`: Blizzard's would error on it.

== 📜 Objectives

|===
| File | Module | Does

| `tracker.lua` | `ObjectiveTracker` | Fades the quest tracker (Questie's or Blizzard's) until hovered
| `capturebars.lua` | `CaptureBars` | PvP capture bars
| `worldstate.lua` | `WorldState` | Battleground scores, zone objectives
| `warnings.lua` | `Warnings` | Error and info messages
| `zone.lua` | `ZoneText` | Turns off Blizzard's zone text
| `pvpemotes.lua` | `PvPEmotes` | An emote on a killing blow
|===

The tracker only changes alpha (allowed in combat): its quest item buttons are
secure.

== 🧭 Minimap

An Ace3 addon kept as it is, inside our folder. It doesn't use the Engine and
nothing calls into it.

|===
| | Engine modules | Minimap

| Framework | Engine | Ace3 (`Libs/`)
| Namespace | `+local ADDON, Engine = ...+` | `+local Addon, ns = ..., DiabolicUIMinimapNS+`
| Saved in | `+DiabolicUI_DB+` | `+DiabolicUI_Minimap_DB+`
|===

Keep the two saved variables apart: the Engine rewrites its keys at every
login and could wipe AceDB's.
