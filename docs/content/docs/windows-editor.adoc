= Windows Editor

Where we tweak how the windows look: open a window, drag its parts, save, `/reload`.

== Start it

[source,bash]
----
tools/editor.py              # needs PySide6, Pillow and lua5.1
tools/install-editor.sh      # optional: adds it to your desktop's menu
----

== The loop

. *In game:* `/dui layout all`, then `/reload`. It opens every window that needs no NPC. Open
  the others yourself while it's on (mail, auction house, bank, vendor, trainer).
. *In the editor:* pick a window at the top, a tab under it, and change what you want. It reads a
  new scan by itself. *Still to scan* on the right says what's missing.
. *Ctrl+S*, then `/reload` in game.

Rescan when a window looks out of date in the editor: its art and texts come from the last scan.

== Change a part

|===
| To | Do

| Pick | Click it, click again for the one under it. Or the list on the left, Ctrl+F to find. Right-click lists all that's there
| Move | Drag it, or arrows (Shift: 10). It snaps to other parts, Alt stops that
| Size | Gold handles, or Ctrl + arrows
| Place it in another part | Drag one of its dots onto that part's point. It stays where it is, and moves with that part from then on
| Stretch it | *Stretch left to right* or *top to bottom* in the panel: its sides stay on what it's in
| Undo | Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z. *Reset* forgets what's not saved of a part
| See better | Wheel to zoom, drag the empty space to pan, F to fit. *Show* turns frames, texts, art and outlines on and off
|===

The list on the left is the window's tree: each part is under what it's placed on, so moving a
part moves what's in it. The *In ...* button in the panel picks what it's in.

A part you change gets a pin (◆) and stays as you set it. Without one it follows the next scan.
*Unpin* gives it back to the scan. Leave unpinned what the game moves by itself.

== Rows of buttons

Pick a button in a row: the panel shows the row. Dragging moves the whole row up or down, its
gold ends change its room.

|===
| Fit | Its buttons

| `fill` | As wide as their texts, the room left shared
| `equal` | All as wide, filling the room
| `center` | As they are, together in the middle
| `ends` | As they are, first and last at the ends
|===

== The foot

A window's buttons and money sit in its foot, so moving the foot moves them. A row of buttons
goes 7 over its bottom and 14 from its sides. It's 35 high: `footer_height` in *Components*, or
`footer` in that window's settings in `settings/blizzard.lua` for a taller one.

== Every window at once

*Components* in the top bar, or *Styled as...* on a part: buttons, scroll bars, dropdowns, the
window itself. Press a control in the sample window and change its values: it shows at once.
Save and `/reload`. The windows show it in the editor after a new `/dui layout`.

== By hand

Each window is a file, `windows/<window>.qml`, with each part inside what it's placed on:

[source,qml]
----
Button {
    name: "MacroDeleteButton"
    text: "Delete"
    pin: "place"
    anchors.left: parent.left
    anchors.leftMargin: 14
    anchors.bottom: parent.bottom
    anchors.bottomMargin: 7
    width: 80
    height: 22
}
----

Then run `lua5.1 tools/positions.lua`. It says what it can't read, and writes
`settings/positions.lua` for the game: don't change that one. Plain values only, anchors on
`parent`. A comment over a part stays.

== What it can't show

It's close to the game, not the game, so check the result in game:

* Rows cut at a list's edge are drawn whole, or not at all.
* Text can wrap a little differently, and its icons aren't drawn.
* 3D models are only boxes.
* What's in a bag and a long list's other rows are drawn but can't be picked.

== Changing the editor itself

`tools/editor.qml` is the editor, `tools/editor.py` its backend, `tools/positions.lua` the tool
behind Save. Rows are worked out twice: `windows/WowRow.qml` shows them, `ResolveRow` in
`tools/positions.lua` writes them, so change both. The tests don't load the QML: after changing
it, open a few windows and try it.
