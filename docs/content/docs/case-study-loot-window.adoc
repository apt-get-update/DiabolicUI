= Case Study: Loot Window

`modules/blizzard/lootframe.lua` replaces Blizzard's loot window. A first
version reskinned Blizzard's `LootFrame`, and kept breaking.

|===
| Reskinning Blizzard's | Building our own

| Guessing frame levels around its clipping | Our frame, our levels
| Reading its widgets to know if a row is used: racy | `GetLootSlotInfo(slot)` is the only truth
| Its scripts crash on our buttons (`LootFrame.page` is nil) | No inherited scripts
| Its layout to measure | A plain list we lay out
|===

== How

++++
{{< mermaid >}}
flowchart LR
    ev["📨 LOOT_OPENED<br/>LOOT_SLOT_CLEARED<br/>LOOT_CLOSED"] -- "unregistered" --> bz["🚫 Blizzard's LootFrame<br/>never opens"]
    ev -- "registered" --> mod["🧩 our module"]
    mod --> api["🔎 GetLootSlotInfo"] --> rows["🪟 our rows"]
{{< /mermaid >}}
++++

. Take Blizzard's loot events from `LootFrame`, register them on the module.
. Make buttons from `LootButtonTemplate`, parented to our frame.
. Fill each row from `GetLootSlotInfo`, every update.
. Stack the rows ourselves.

== Template gotchas

|===
| Problem | Fix

| Its normal, highlight and pushed textures show on hover | `button:SetNormalTexture("")` etc., hide other regions
| Its `OnUpdate` calls `+LootItem_OnEnter+`, which needs `LootFrame.page` | `button:SetScript("OnUpdate", nil)`
| The icon and text aren't fields | `+_G[name .. "IconTexture"]+`, `+_G[name .. "Text"]+`
|===

The row backgrounds stretch with the item name: scale the whole
link:{{< relref "conventions#texture-canvas" >}}[texture canvas], not just its art.
