= Case Study: Loot Window

Why we build our own frames. `modules/blizzard/lootframe.lua` first reskinned Blizzard's `LootFrame`,
and kept breaking.

|===
| Reskinning | Our own

| Guessing frame levels around its clipping | Our frame, our levels
| Reading its widgets to know if a row is used: racy | `GetLootSlotInfo(slot)` is the only truth
| Its scripts crash on our buttons (`LootFrame.page` is nil) | No inherited scripts
|===

So: take its loot events off `LootFrame` and register them on the module, make rows from
`LootButtonTemplate` parented to our frame, fill them from `GetLootSlotInfo` on every update.

`button:SetNormalTexture("")` hides the template's art, `button:SetScript("OnUpdate", nil)` stops its
crash, and its icon and text are `+_G[name .. "IconTexture"]+`, `+_G[name .. "Text"]+`.
