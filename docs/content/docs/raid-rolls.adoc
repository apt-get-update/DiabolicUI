= Raid Rolls

Two windows, off unless `rollWindow` is on (`/dui config`, Master Loot Rolls). They read the master looter's
chat, whatever addon wrote it, so raiders need nothing else.

|===
| File | Shows

| `rollwindow.lua` | The item up for a roll, its time left, then your roll. From uncommon up, the icon's border, a glow and the window's border take the quality's color. The buttons are Blizzard's dice (Main Spec, `/roll 100`), coin (Off Spec, `/roll 99`) and our X (Pass)
| `lootpreview.lua` | The boss's loot still to roll, the item being rolled in gold. It grows to its longest name
|===

++++

`CanPutUpRolls` in `rollwindow.lua` says who's trusted: a raid warning or the leader always, raid and
party chat from the master looter or an assistant.

|===
| Message | Does

| A link and "roll" | Adds a row; "20s" sets its time
| A link and "wins", "awarded", "cancel"... | Removes its row
| "roll" and a number, no link | The last row's time
| `Loot from Boss: [a] [b]`, `Boss dropped 2 items:` | Fills the preview
|===

Anyone with DiabolicUI who opens a corpse under master loot also sends the items as addon messages
(prefix `DiabolicUI`).

Both are light popups in the loot window's look (`loot` in `settings/blizzard.lua`), no title
plate or red band: those are for big windows.

*To try it:* `/dui loot`. Tests: `tests/test_session_rollwindow.lua`.
